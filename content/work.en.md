This is not a complete CV. It explains where the writing meets resistance: the systems I have entered, the responsibilities I have carried, and the places where I have had to become a beginner again.

## Secondary education in New Zealand

I am a registered teacher in New Zealand. The classroom brings learning differences, cultural differences, institutional constraints, and new questions created by technology into daily contact. Education is not simply a specialist topic for me; it is a place to observe how resources, opportunity, and human growth interact.

## Astronomy and public service

I serve as Executive Secretary of the Royal Astronomical Society of New Zealand and contribute to New Zealand astronomy olympiad work. These roles keep me thinking about how professional communities function, how volunteers collaborate, and how scientific curiosity becomes a real opportunity for young people.

## Applied computing and learning again

I am studying toward a master's degree in applied computing. Learning programming from the foundations in midlife places me on both sides of education—as a teacher and a beginner—and gives me a lived view of how artificial intelligence is changing learning, work, and the value of individual capability.

## Finance and international development

Before moving to New Zealand, I worked in roles connected with Deloitte Beijing, Sinosure, and the Export-Import Bank of China, including time living and working in Central Asia. That experience exposed me to risk, institutions, national development, and cross-cultural cooperation. It also taught me early that individual effort is never the sole explanation for social outcomes.

## Grand Tour

Beyond migration, I have visited 42 countries. The map records the places; the writing is more interested in what happened to a person while moving among them.

[Explore the Grand Tour](/en/grand-tour/)

These experiences will not become four equal content silos. They all serve one line of inquiry: how an ordinary person without inherited advantage keeps rebuilding a life in the real world.
