My experience may look scattered: a small town, a large city, finance, Central Asia, New Zealand, education, astronomy, and computing. What connects these places is not “career variety” for its own sake, but the question of how an ordinary person preserves agency while the surrounding world keeps changing.

These four themes form the map of the project. Each new essay begins in one of them, but they all return to the same concern: how a person creates room to choose again within the limits of circumstance.
