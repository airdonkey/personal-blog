I come from an ordinary family, and I do not want to present travel as a ledger of consumption. Within the choices available to me, I have repeatedly chosen to exchange part of my savings for distance, unfamiliar languages, and things seen first-hand.

This archive records more than where I have been. It follows how an ordinary person deliberately enlarges his world. The map preserves coordinates, photographs preserve the scene, and the writing asks what I actually saw after leaving familiar ground—and what changed because of it.
