---
title: "Side Paths"
description: "Short notes left by books, film, astronomy, maps, and everyday curiosities."
translationKey: "notes"
---

Not every thought needs to be enlarged into a grand conclusion. This is where I keep details worth preserving before they become essays: the afterlife of a book, an association prompted by a map, a memory released by music, or a question that has suddenly become clearer.

Side Paths are written with the same care as the essays, but they do different work. Notes preserve the moment a thought first appears; a few may later grow into complete arguments.
