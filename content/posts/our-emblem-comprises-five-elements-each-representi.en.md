Our emblem comprises five elements, each representing an educational philosophy:

The Spark: Learning begins with the awakening of understanding, not the input of information. Without this spark, even the most perfect structure cannot truly function.

The Vessel: Beneath the flame lies the structure that holds it. Knowledge must be housed and organised to be truly effective—this is the very essence of ‘capacity’.

The Laurel Wreath: The wreath encircling it symbolises that learning is not a one-off achievement, but an evolutionary process of cognition that deepens over time.

The Knot: Binding the whole together, this represents the most fundamental skill of a liberal arts education—establishing connections between different bodies of knowledge to form a holistic understanding.

The Stars: Above the flame lie distant points of light. They symbolise realms yet to be understood, as well as the ever-expanding boundaries of cognition. The purpose of education lies not in reaching those boundaries, but in continually expanding our capacity for understanding.
