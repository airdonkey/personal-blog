I spent many years in finance and international development before transitioning to education and astronomy. I currently work as a registered teacher in New Zealand and serve as Executive Secretary of the Royal Astronomical Society of New Zealand.

## Career Path

My career trajectory has been anything but linear. Starting as an auditor at Deloitte Beijing Office, I moved into risk analysis at Sinosure before becoming a founding member of the Export-Import Bank of China's Liaison Team in Central Asia and Transcaucasia.

In the mid-2010s, I left the financial sector, relocated to New Zealand, and transitioned into education. I now teach in New Zealand while managing administrative operations for the Royal Astronomical Society of New Zealand.

## Areas of Interest

- **Cross-Cultural Observation** — Living and working in China, Central Asia, and Oceania has cultivated an ongoing interest in how different societies function
- **Comparative Education** — Exploring what can be learned from different educational systems
- **Astronomy & Earth Sciences** — A long-standing personal interest that has become part of my professional life

## Why I Write

This blog primarily documents patterns, contrasts, and insights I observe across different cultures and professional domains. I don't offer ready-made answers or promote any particular methodology—just observations from someone who shifted from finance to education and science.

I write for those who understand that careers need not follow conventional paths and who maintain an interest in lifelong learning.
